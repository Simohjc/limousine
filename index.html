Company Policies & Compliance

Important information about safety, accessibility, and how to report concerns.

Zero‑Tolerance Substance Policy

KB Transportation strives to protect public safety and has instituted a strict zero tolerance substance policy. Drivers are strictly banned from using intoxicating substances when providing transportation services.
If you believe your driver may be under the influence of drugs and/or alcohol, please have the driver end the trip immediately. The driver must report to the office upon ending the trip. If the driver tests positive, he or she will be subject to immediate removal. The company periodically checks and updates its policy.
KB Transportation Management

ADA Compliance

KB Transportation is committed to full compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).
We ensure that our services are accessible to all passengers, including those with disabilities.
Wheelchair‑accessible vehicles are available upon request. We make reasonable modifications to our policies, practices, and procedures to accommodate individuals with disabilities.
If you require assistance or special accommodations, please contact us so we can ensure a safe and comfortable ride.
KB Transportation Management

Feedback & Complaints

Your satisfaction and safety matter to us. If you have any complaints regarding your ride, driver conduct, accessibility issues, or any other concern, you may file a grievance with the City of Dallas.
